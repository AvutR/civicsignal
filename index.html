Report details

    Your data, on your device

    This prototype stores report text in local storage and photos/audio in this browser's media database. No reports are submitted to a server or public authority, and no account is required. Other visitors cannot see your reports.

    Avoid entering private or sensitive information. Clearing browser data removes your reports. JSON export includes report text and attachment metadata; photo and audio files are not embedded in the export.

    Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap; fonts are loaded from Google. The map library is bundled locally. These services receive ordinary web requests. Your location is requested only when you choose “Use my location”.

    Status changes are a demo workflow, not an official response. A shared service would need authentication, moderation, a database, and an agreed privacy policy.

    Reset this demo?

    This removes locally added reports and status changes, then restores the sample data. Export anything you want to keep first.
